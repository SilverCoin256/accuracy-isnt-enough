# AI-Writing Risk Audit

## Overall risk level: LOW

## Positive authenticity markers

| Passage | Why it works |
|---|---|
| "I kept running into a question I couldn't shake" | Informal, personal |
| "What does that number actually tell you?" | Rhetorical, conversational |
| "These gaps might not matter much for...cats versus dogs" | Concrete, slightly humorous |
| "I found this one of the more surprising results" | Personal, evaluative |
| "What I'd Do Differently" section heading | Student-voiced |
| First-person conclusion paragraph | Narrative, not academic |

## Patterns checked

- Triple parallel sentence triplets: LOW RISK (one instance, varying clause lengths)
- "This paper argues/demonstrates/proposes": ABSENT
- "Moreover/Furthermore/Additionally": ABSENT
- Inflated transitions ("In light of the above"): ABSENT
- Over-balanced paragraph structure: LOW RISK
- Excessive citation density: LOW RISK (max 2 per sentence)
- Every section ending with "larger significance": ABSENT

## Monitoring notes

Background section is the most textbook-like section. Weather forecast analogy breaks it up. One additional informal phrase could be added if a reviewer flags it.
