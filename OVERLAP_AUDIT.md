# Overlap Audit

Checks material overlap between this paper and four empirically related prior manuscripts.

## Key findings

- **No prose overlap**: All text written from scratch with student-investigative framing
- **Shared elements**: IBM HR dataset, CatBoost, AUC/ECE/fairness metrics -- these are unavoidable when replicating experiments on the same benchmark
- **Absent from NHSJS**: GADRS composite score, SSI, zone-level calibration, nine-stage framework, proxy audit, SHAP stability, metric bounding, evaluation discipline framing, EU AI Act language
- **Different contribution**: Prior papers claim governance/deployment frameworks; this paper claims educational demonstration of co-occurring failures

## Terminology collision check

All proprietary framing terms from prior papers (GADRS, SSI, AURC, metric scope violations, eight-stage correction, oversight saturation) are ABSENT from the NHSJS manuscript.

## Self-plagiarism risk: LOW

Shared numerical results from the same dataset are replication, not plagiarism. No sentence-level prose is reused. Structural architecture is different. Audience, scope, and contribution claim are distinct.
